# AllGo Multi-Vertical Product & Feature Taxonomy

## 1. Executive Summary

AllGo operates as a unified multi-vertical platform in India. Rather than isolating food, rides, and courier into separate disconnected applications, AllGo consolidates customer discovery, courier fulfillment, and merchant operations into a single shared backend and unified partner fleet.

---

## 2. Vertical Taxonomy & Operational Specifications

### 2.1 Hyperlocal Food Delivery
- **Vertical Code:** `FOOD`
- **Fulfillment Modes:** 
  - **Mode A (Separate):** Standard parallel dispatch. Multiple independent couriers assigned to individual restaurants.
  - **Mode B (Combined):** Proximity corridor bundling (outlets within 1.5 km, total delivery distance ≤ 5 km, courier bonus ₹20, customer discount ₹25).
- **Menu Localization:** Bilingual at launch — English and Tamil (Hindi in phase 2). All merchant item names, descriptions, and dietary tags (`VEG`, `NON_VEG`, `EGG`) fully localized in `item_translations`.
- **Preparation & Dispatch:** JIT dispatch. Order status transitions from `CONFIRMED` -> `PREPARING` -> `READY_FOR_PICKUP` -> `DISPATCHED` -> `DELIVERED`.
- **Packaging Fees:** Per-outlet packaging fee itemized separately with statutory GST.

### 2.2 Hyperlocal Grocery & Essentials
- **Vertical Code:** `GROCERY`
- **Catalog Model:** SKU-based inventory with real-time stock checks (`inventory` and `inventory_reservations` with 10-minute quote TTL).
- **Perishability & Sequencing:** In Mode B combined runs, grocery pickups are strictly sequenced AFTER food pickups to minimize ambient temperature exposure for fresh produce and dairy.
- **Physical Limits:** Max combined weight 12 kg; max combined volume 30 Liters per 2-wheeler courier leg.

### 2.3 Intra-City Parcel Courier
- **Vertical Code:** `PARCEL`
- **Operational Model:** On-demand point-to-point courier service (custom package pickup and drop).
- **Package Categories:** Documents, Clothing, Personal Items, Food Boxes, Electronics. Restricted items (flammables, alcohol, currency) rejected at booking.
- **Security & Dual OTP:**
  - **Pickup OTP:** 6-digit cryptographic numeric OTP generated by sender and verified by partner before accepting package.
  - **Delivery OTP:** 6-digit numeric OTP generated by recipient and verified by partner before final handover.
- **Pricing:** Base fare + per-kilometer slab + optional transit insurance fee.

### 2.4 Passenger Mobility (Rides)
- **Vertical Code:** `RIDES`
- **Vehicle Categories:**
  - `BIKE`: Rapid low-cost single passenger mobility.
  - `AUTO`: 3-wheeler metered-rate commuter transit.
  - `CAB_ECONOMY`: 4-seater hatchback / compact sedan with A/C.
  - `CAB_PREMIUM`: 4-to-6-seater executive sedan / SUV.
- **Safety & Trip Verification:** Start OTP verified by driver before trip initiation; SOS / emergency escalation integration; real-time route deviation telemetry.
- **Fare Composition:** Base fare + Distance rate (₹/km) + Time rate (₹/min) + Surge multiplier + Platform fee + 5% GST.

### 2.5 Designated Driver Booking (Hire-Driver)
- **Vertical Code:** `HIRE_DRIVER`
- **Operational Model:** Hourly booking of a professional driver to operate the customer's personal vehicle.
- **Vehicle Classification:** Manual transmission vs Automatic transmission eligibility matching.
- **Booking Durations:** 4-hour, 8-hour, and 12-hour hourly slabs, with overtime increments (₹150/hr). Night allowance (₹200) applied between 22:00 and 06:00.
- **Lifecycle Verification:** Start OTP and End OTP for precise time tracking and odometer verification.
